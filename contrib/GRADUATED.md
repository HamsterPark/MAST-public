# Graduated skills · 已毕业的技能

A community skill graduates once a maintainer has verified it on a real instrument. It then leaves
`contrib/` for the official tree (`MASTv2/mast/skills/builtins/` or `config/composite_skills/`), its major
version is bumped, and it is listed here so the credit stays with the people who wrote it.

社区技能经维护者真机验证后毕业：离开 `contrib/`、进入官方树（`MASTv2/mast/skills/builtins/` 或
`config/composite_skills/`），主版本号 +1，并记在这里 —— 署名留给写它的人。
